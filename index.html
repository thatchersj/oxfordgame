    1. Goal & Uncommitted Lines

    Visit all 6 Souvenir cards and be first back to the Sheldonian Theatre. At the start—or after Hazard cards teleport you—you are uncommitted and may roll first, then travel along any connected route.

    2. Movement & Rolling a 6

    Roll the die and move up to that number of stops. Rolling a 6 lets you pass through closed stops, reopening them and letting you immediately relocate those blockers anywhere on the board.

    3. Interchanges & Souvenirs

    Stopping at a white interchange lets you switch to another intersecting line by drawing a Hazard card—even if that stop also completes one of your Souvenir destinations!

    4. Blockers

    A blocker may be placed on any station except the Sheldonian, a station already blocked, or a station where any player is currently standing. Souvenir destinations are not protected and may be blocked.
